# KADSAMHSA Learning Management System (LMS)

**Quick Summary:**
A comprehensive online learning platform enabling self-paced course enrolment, interactive assessments, and verifiable certificate issuance for KADSAMHSA's training programs.

## What We Do

We built an LMS that brings KADSAMHSA's training curricula online—enabling individuals and partner organizations to self-enrol in courses, complete quizzes, and earn digital certificates that can be verified independently. The platform shifts training delivery from in-person workshops to scalable, self-service online learning.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/ui
- **Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage with Row-Level Security)
- **Integrations:** Paystack (payments), Resend (email), PDF generation
- **Deployment:** Vercel

## Platform Features

- **Learner Portal:** Browse courses, self-enrol, track progress, attempt quizzes, download certificates
- **Admin Dashboard:** Create and publish courses, manage users and organizations, set pricing, view reports
- **Organizations:** Invite staff to complete training with org-scoped access and certificates
- **Verification:** Public certificate verification endpoint for employers/partners
- **Responsive Design:** Full mobile support for on-the-go learning

## Hosted On

Vercel (serverless deployment)

## Launch Content

_Sensitization on Drug Use, Drug Dependence and Drug Prevention, Treatment and Care (DPTC)_ — an EU/UNODC-supported curriculum adapted for law enforcement and public training in Nigeria.
